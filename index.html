          About us

          Who we are, what we do and how we are paid.

          See the seat before the night

          Atvents keeps track of the big concerts in Jakarta and builds a 3D model of each venue, seat by seat, with the stage and the screens of the show that plays there. Anyone can look round the venue from above. For Rp25.000, a fan fills in their seat, name and email, pays, and a guided tour takes them from the whole venue to their category, then to the view from their own seat, and makes a story video of it.

          What we are not

          We do not sell concert tickets. Each concert page names the official seller (tiket.com, Loket, TipTip or Traveloka), who sells the ticket, takes the payment and sends the e-ticket. We are not the seller, the promoter or the artist. Their names and posters are used to show which concert a page is about.

          How we are paid

          We earn from the 3D seat view: Rp25.000 per seat, per concert. Some links to tiket.com are affiliate links, and tiket.com may pay us a commission on a sale, at no cost to you. We also license the 3D seat view to ticketing platforms, venues and promoters; see for partners.

          Talk to us

          Questions about your 3D pass, the site or working together: contact. Questions about a concert ticket go to its seller.

          Privacy policy

          Last updated 18 November 2026.

          This policy explains what Atvents (“we”) collects when you use this site, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). Write to us at with any question.

          What we collect, and why

          When you click a link to a ticket seller

          For tiket.com links: a random click code, the concert, the time, and the campaign that brought you here if the link had one. No name and no email. The click code travels to tiket.com in the link and comes back to us in the affiliate report when a sale is made.

          When you unlock a 3D view

          Your name, your email, the concert, the category and seat you choose, and the reference of your payment. We use them to open your pass, send you its link and receipt, and answer you if something goes wrong. If you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft, we take only your name and email address from that account, as if you had typed them. We get no password, contacts or anything else from it, and keep nothing from the sign-in itself.

          When you pay for a 3D view

          The payment provider handles the Rp25.000 payment and tells us only that it was paid, with its reference. We never see your card, wallet or bank details.

          When you show your Instagram to seat mates

          Only if you choose to, from the 3D view of a paid pass: the name you type and your Instagram username, kept with your seat. Fans with a paid 3D pass for the same concert see them as a tag over your seat, so you can meet before the show. They never see your email, the name on your order or your order number. You can take them down at any time from the same place, and they are gone for everyone at once.

          When you ask about working with us

          Your name, company, email, phone number, website and message, to answer you.

          When you sign up for alerts

          Your email, to send concert alerts. Every email has a way to leave the list.

          On your device

          Your 3D passes, your click codes and the campaign you came from are kept in your browser’s storage, so the site remembers them. You can clear them in your browser at any time. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

          Who else sees it

          tiket.com and its affiliate network see the click code in the link you follow, along with what they collect under their own privacy policies when you buy. They do not get your name or email from us. The payment provider, Midtrans, gets your name and email with your order and processes your payment under its own policy. The company that hosts our server stores our data for us, and our email service, Resend, sends you your pass link. The site’s fonts and its 3D viewer are served from this site, not from Google Fonts or a code CDN. If you continue with Google or Microsoft, that company sees that you signed in to us, under its own privacy policy. Fans with a paid pass for the same concert see the name and Instagram you chose to show them, and nothing else. We do not sell personal data.

          How long we keep it

          E-tickets you send: until the concert has taken place. Click records and 3D pass details: until twelve months after the concert. What you show to seat mates: until you take it down, and no later than one month after the concert. Partner requests: two years after our last contact. Alerts: until you leave the list.

          Your rights

          You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to withdraw your consent, or to object to how we use it. Email us from the address you used and we answer within 3 × 24 hours of a complete request, as the law sets out.

          Security

          The site runs over HTTPS. Only our administrators can read pass and partner data, behind a secret key that never appears in the site’s code.

          Changes

          When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced on the home page.

          Terms of use

          Last updated 30 October 2026.

          1. What we do

          Atvents lists concerts, links to the official seller of each, and sells a 3D view of the venue from your seat. We are not the ticket seller, the promoter or the artist.

          2. Your ticket

          When you buy a concert ticket, your contract is with its seller and the promoter, on their terms. They set prices and availability and handle payment, e-tickets, changes, refunds and cancellations. Prices on our pages are as announced and can be out of date; the seller’s price is the one that counts.

          3. The 3D view

          The 3D venue is our illustration, built from the venue and the category layout of the show. Distances, angles and sight lines are close, not exact, and the stage, screens and crowd on the night will differ. The view does not promise you a particular seat or view: your ticket decides your seat.

          4. 3D passes

          A 3D pass costs Rp25.000 and opens the view from one seat at one concert; 3D Pass Plus costs Rp49.000 (Rp24.000 as an upgrade of a paid seat pass) and opens every seat of one concert. Both stay open after the show. It opens as soon as the payment goes through. It is a preview of the venue and does not include a concert ticket or guarantee a seat. We refund it if the show is cancelled. We may close a pass that is shared or misused.

          5. Fair use

          Do not copy, scrape or extract the 3D models, share pass links publicly, send automated requests, or try to get round the payment.

          6. Ownership

          The site and its 3D models belong to us. Artists’ names, posters and logos belong to their owners and are shown only to identify the concerts.

          7. Liability

          The site is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for tickets bought from sellers, for changes to a show, or for decisions made on the basis of the 3D view.

          8. Law

          These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.
