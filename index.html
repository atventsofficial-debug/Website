        About us

        Who we are, what we do and how we are paid.

        See the seat before the night

        Atvents lists the big concerts in Jakarta and builds a 3D model of each venue, seat by seat, with the stage and the screens of the show that plays there. Anyone can look round the venue from above. Fans who buy their tickets on tiket.com through our links send us their seat and e-ticket; once we have checked it, a guided tour takes them from the whole venue to their category, then to the view from their own seat, and makes a story video of it. A ticket bought elsewhere can join for Rp25.000.

        What we are not

        We do not sell tickets. tiket.com sells the tickets, takes the payment and sends the e-ticket, as if you had gone there yourself. We are not tiket.com, and we are not the promoters or the artists. Their names and posters are used to show which concert a page is about.

        How we are paid

        When you buy on tiket.com through one of our links, tiket.com may pay us a commission through its affiliate program. You pay the same price either way. Fans who bought elsewhere pay us Rp25.000 for a seat view. We also license the 3D seat view to ticketing platforms, venues and promoters; see for partners.

        Privacy policy

        Last updated 30 October 2026.

        This policy explains what Atvents (“we”) collects when you use this site, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). Write to us at with any question.

        What we collect, and why

        When you click Buy on tiket.com

        A random click code, the concert, the time, and the campaign that brought you here if the link had one. No name and no email. The click code travels to tiket.com in the link, and comes back to us in the affiliate report when a sale is made, so we know which sales came from our links.

        When you unlock a 3D view

        Your tiket.com order ID, the email you used there, the category and seat you choose, the e-ticket you send, and the click codes saved on your device for that concert. We use them to check that the e-ticket matches the seat, to match the sale with the affiliate report, and to write to you about the pass. A person on our team looks at the e-ticket only to check it. We never ask for your payment details.

        When you pay for a seat view

        If you bought your ticket elsewhere and pay Rp25.000 for the seat view, the payment provider handles the payment and tells us only that it was paid, with its reference. We never see your card, wallet or bank details.

        When you ask about working with us

        Your name, company, email, phone number, website and message, to answer you.

        When you sign up for alerts

        Your email, to send concert alerts. Every email has a way to leave the list.

        On your device

        Your 3D passes, your click codes and the campaign you came from are kept in your browser’s storage, so the site remembers them. You can clear them in your browser at any time. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

        Who else sees it

        tiket.com and its affiliate network see the click code in the link you follow, along with what they collect under their own privacy policies when you buy. They do not get your email or order ID from us. The company that hosts our server stores our data for us. The site’s fonts load from Google Fonts and the 3D viewer from jsDelivr, so those services see your IP address. We do not sell personal data.

        How long we keep it

        E-tickets you send: until the concert has taken place. Click records and 3D pass details: until twelve months after the concert. Partner requests: two years after our last contact. Alerts: until you leave the list.

        Your rights

        You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to withdraw your consent, or to object to how we use it. Email us from the address you used and we answer within 3 × 24 hours of a complete request, as the law sets out.

        Security

        The site runs over HTTPS. Only our administrators can read pass and partner data, behind a secret key that never appears in the site’s code.

        Changes

        When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced on the home page.

        Terms of use

        Last updated 30 October 2026.

        1. What we do

        Atvents lists concerts, links to tiket.com where the tickets are sold, and gives a 3D view of the venue. We are not the seller, the promoter or tiket.com.

        2. Your ticket

        When you buy, your contract is with tiket.com and the promoter, on their terms. They set prices and availability and handle payment, e-tickets, changes, refunds and cancellations. Prices on our pages are as announced and can be out of date; the price on tiket.com is the one that counts.

        3. The 3D view

        The 3D venue is our illustration, built from the venue and the category layout of the show. Distances, angles and sight lines are close, not exact, and the stage, screens and crowd on the night will differ. The view does not promise you a particular seat or view: your ticket decides your seat.

        4. 3D passes

        The view from a seat is for the person who holds the ticket for it. One e-ticket opens one pass, for the seat printed on it. We check every pass by hand against the e-ticket before the seat tour opens, and we may close a pass that does not match, or one that is shared or misused. A pass is free with a ticket bought through our links. For a ticket bought elsewhere, the seat view costs Rp25.000 for one concert; we refund it if the show is cancelled, or if we cannot approve a genuine ticket.

        5. Fair use

        Do not copy, scrape or extract the 3D models, claim passes for bookings that are not yours, send automated requests, or try to get round the checks.
